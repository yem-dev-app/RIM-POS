# POS - Smart Point of Sale & Billing System

A comprehensive, offline-first Flutter application designed for seamless business management. This POS system empowers retail stores, grocery shops, pharmacies, and small businesses with powerful tools for billing, inventory, and accounting.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛒 POS & Billing
- **Fast order creation** for efficient customer checkout.
- **Barcode scanning support** for quick product lookup.
- **Discounts, tax & multiple payment methods** integration.
- **Instant receipt generation** for every transaction.

### 📦 Inventory Management
- **Real-time stock tracking** to prevent stockouts.
- **Low stock alerts** to notify when it's time to reorder.
- **Category-based product organization** for easy navigation.
- **Auto stock updates** after every sale or purchase.

### 🏪 Multi-Store Support
- **Manage multiple stores** easily from a single application.
- **Separate inventory & reports** for each location.
- **Store-level customization** to suit different business needs.

### 📊 Reports & Analytics
- **Sales reports** to track revenue trends.
- **Expense tracking** for better financial control.
- **Profit insights** to understand business health.
- **Business performance overview** at a glance.

### 📑 Accounting & Ledger
- **Double-entry accounting system** for accuracy.
- **Customer & supplier balances** tracking.
- **Expense management** integrated with billing.

### ☁️ Backup & Security
- **Google Drive backup & restore** for data safety.
- **Offline-first SQLite storage** ensures functionality without internet.
- **Secure authentication** powered by Firebase.

### ⚡ Advanced Features
- **Works offline & online** seamlessly.
- **Barcode-to-cart system** for modern retail experience.
- **Multi-device sync-ready** architecture.
- **Thermal receipt printing** via Bluetooth or Network.
- **Dynamic payment system** supporting various gateways.
- **Optimized high-performance database** for speed.
